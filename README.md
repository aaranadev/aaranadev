## About me

Hi, I'm **Alberto Arana** — a Principal Software Engineer. I design and ship scalable systems, from frontend platforms to high-performance backends, with a bias for clarity and calm precision.

I enjoy the unglamorous parts of engineering: drawing clean boundaries, removing accidental complexity, and leaving a codebase calmer than I found it. I work remotely and in both English and Spanish.

## What I focus on

- **Scalable architecture** — turning fuzzy requirements into systems that stay simple as they grow.
- **Frontend platforms** — React & Next.js (SSR/RSC), design systems, and microfrontends that let teams ship independently.
- **High-performance backends** — Node.js services, REST/GraphQL APIs, and the performance/observability work that keeps them healthy.
- **Type safety end to end** — strict TypeScript, expressive types, and contracts that catch mistakes before runtime.
- **Developer experience** — tooling, conventions, and docs that make the right thing the easy thing.

## How I work

- **Clarity over cleverness** — code is read far more than it's written; I optimize for the reader.
- **Small, reversible steps** — ship in thin slices, keep the build green, learn quickly.
- **Accessibility and performance are features**, not afterthoughts — this very site targets 100/100 on both.
- **Calm precision** — measured decisions, honest trade-offs, and no drama in the pipeline.
